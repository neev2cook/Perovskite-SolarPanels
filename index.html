Why perovskite?

Ultra‑high efficiency

Perovskite cells surpass silicon limits, reaching >30% in lab.

Perovskite solar cells have achieved certified efficiencies above 31% for tandem configurations, and over 25% for single-junction cells. This is possible because perovskites can absorb a broader spectrum of sunlight and have a tunable bandgap.

31.2% tandem record 25.7% single junction 23% flexible
  • Tandem with silicon: 31.2% record (EPFL, 2023) — combines perovskite top cell with silicon bottom cell.
  • Single junction: 25.7% (UNIST, 2022) — already competitive with commercial silicon.
  • Flexible & lightweight: efficiency retained at 23% on plastic substrates.
  • All-perovskite tandem: theoretical limit >40% — the next frontier.

Rapid progress: efficiency jumped from 3.8% (2009) to 31.2% (2023) — the fastest advancement in solar history.

Perovskites can be tuned to absorb different parts of the light spectrum, making them ideal for multi-junction cells.

Low‑cost production

Solution‑processable & earth‑abundant materials.

Perovskite films can be printed or spin-coated at low temperatures (<150°C), dramatically reducing manufacturing energy and cost compared to silicon (which requires >1400°C).

10–20× cheaper materials <1 yr payback $0.02–0.04 /kWh LCOE
  • Material cost: 10–20× cheaper than silicon — perovskite precursors are earth-abundant and inexpensive.
  • Roll-to-roll: compatible with high-throughput printing on flexible substrates.
  • Low temperature: <150°C processing vs >1400°C for silicon — massive energy savings.
  • Payback time: under 1 year in sunny regions (vs 3–5 years for silicon).

Levelized cost of energy (LCOE) projected at $0.02–0.04/kWh — among the cheapest electricity sources ever.

Manufacturing can be scaled rapidly using existing printing infrastructure, accelerating global deployment.

Tunable bandgap

Perfect for tandem cells & flexible, lightweight panels.

The perovskite bandgap can be tuned from 1.2 eV to 2.3 eV by changing the halide composition (I⁻, Br⁻, Cl⁻). This enables:

1.2–2.3 eV tunable range >40% theoretical tandem BIPV & wearables
  • Tandem cells: perovskite top cell + silicon bottom cell — already >31% efficiency.
  • All-perovskite tandem: two perovskite layers with different bandgaps — >40% theoretical limit.
  • Colored & transparent: for BIPV (building-integrated PV) — windows that generate power.
  • Flexible substrates: power for IoT, wearables, drones, and portable electronics.

Ideal partner for silicon — perovskite/silicon tandems are already breaking records and are expected to dominate the next generation of solar panels.

Bandgap tuning also allows semi-transparent panels for agrivoltaics — growing crops under solar panels.
